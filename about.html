🌸 About Buttercup Bakes 🌸

Buttercup Bakes is a student-run business that started in 2023. We are passionate about creating delicious homemade treats that bring joy to our customers. Our mission is to provide high-quality baked goods made with love and care. Whether you're craving a sweet indulgence or a savory delight, Buttercup Bakes has something for everyone. Join us as we spread happiness one bite at a time.

🌟 Vision

To become the go-to destination for homemade treats in our community. We aim to create a warm, welcoming environment and expand to cater to all dietary preferences — bringing joy and comfort through every bite.

🎯 Mission

To provide high-quality, homemade treats using finest ingredients and traditional techniques. We spread happiness one bite at a time and make every customer feel special.
